# ClownPierce's Scythe 🩸

[![Modrinth Downloads](https://shields.io)](https://modrinth.com/mod/clownscythe)
[![Platform](https://shields.io)](https://fabricmc.net)
[![Minecraft Version](https://shields.io)](https://minecraft.net)

A **ClownPierce-themed Fabric mod** inspired by Doctor4t, adding a custom scythe, blood mechanics, custom enchantments, a boss dungeon, and more. Designed to pair with the official ClownPierce-themed texture pack.

---

## ⚙️ Requirements & Compatibility

- **Minecraft Version:** 1.20.1 – 1.20.6
- **Platform:** Fabric (Client & Server)
- **Dependencies:** 
  - [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net) (0.14.22+)
  - [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com)
  - Java 17+
- **Recommended:** [Mod Menu](https://modrinth.com)

---

## 🚀 Features

### ⚔️ The Clown's Scythe
* **Melee Combat:** Features 500 durability, pulls enemies toward you on hit, and includes lifesteal.
* **Dynamic Tooltip:** Tracks kill counts, progression, Blood Aura status, and Blood Beam data.

### 📈 Kill Progression & Blood Auras
* **Blood Aura I (50 Kills):** Grants Strength I, Speed I, Resistance I, and custom particles.
* **Blood Aura II (100 Kills):** Upgrades to Strength II, Speed II, Resistance I, and faster regeneration.

### 🔴 Blood Beam
* Right-click to fire a 20-block long projectile (14 damage, costs 1.5 hearts) that transfers your negative status effects (e.g., Poison, Wither) to the target.

### 📜 Custom Enchantments
* Includes scythe-exclusive enchantments such as **Bloodlust**, **Pull**, **Executioner**, **Crowd Reaper**, **Blood Mark**, and **Harvest Rush**.

---

## 🏰 The Clown Boss Dungeon

Find the **Blood Compass** in desert pyramids to locate an underground dungeon complex:
* **5 Themed Floors:** Fight through storage rooms, prison cells, labs, catacombs, and a final temple.
* **Bosses:** Face wave encounters culminating in the **Iron Warden** and the final multi-phase boss, the **Scythe Guardian** (500 HP).
* **Loot:** Rewards include the Clown's Scythe and pre-enchanted Netherite Armor.
